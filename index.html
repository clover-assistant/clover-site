Clover

Clover is a private, on-device household assistant. It runs on a single computer inside one family's home and is not offered as a service to the public.

How it is used

Its owners connect their own accounts — such as Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive and Notion — so that Clover can help with everyday household tasks: reading a calendar, adding a note, or updating a shared list. Access is granted by the account owner through each provider's own authorization flow.

Privacy

Clover stores its data locally on the owner's device. It does not sell data, does not share it with third parties, and does not operate an advertising or analytics service.
