Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Clover is a private household assistant that runs entirely on a computer owned by its users. This policy explains what the Clover application ("the app") does with the data it is granted access to through Google APIs and other connected services.

Who operates the app

The app is operated by its owners for personal, non-commercial household use. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not distributed to the public.

What data the app accesses

Only after an account holder explicitly grants permission through Google's OAuth consent screen, the app may access:

  • Google Calendar — to read, create and update calendar events on request.
  • Gmail — to read messages and to send messages when asked.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — to read and write documents and spreadsheets the account holder points it at.
  • Contacts — read-only, to resolve names when the account holder asks.

Where data is stored

Data obtained through these APIs is stored locally on the owner's own device, in a private data directory accessible only to the app's system account. Credentials and access tokens are stored in that same local directory with file permissions restricted to the app's own user. The app does not operate a server, does not send this data to any third party, and does not use any advertising, analytics or tracking service.

How data is used

Data is used solely to respond to the account holder's requests inside their own household — for example, reading a calendar to answer a question, or drafting a message the account holder asked for. It is not used to train machine-learning models, is not sold, and is not shared.

Third-party processing

To generate responses, the app may send relevant excerpts of your request to the large-language-model provider the owner has configured, under that provider's own terms. Google user data is only sent to that provider when it is needed to answer a specific request from the account holder.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the owner's device until the owner deletes it. Deleting the app's data directory removes conversations, cached content and stored tokens. Account holders can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the app's stored tokens.

Limited Use disclosure

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is used within a single household by its owners. It does not create accounts and does not knowingly collect information from anyone outside that household.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
